TOP 5 ARCHITECTURAL STRENGTHS
------------------------------

2. Middleware Pattern - Clean functional chain with logging, metrics, validation
3. Orchestrator Pattern - Thread-safe coordinator with retry logic, rate limiting

5. Observability - Structured logging (zerolog), Prometheus metrics, event store
